## Hollywood as a graph

Inspired by a TikTok, this ten-hour experiment asks how tightly connected Hollywood actors are when movies become the links. The claim was that any two actors in IMDb’s top-1000 list could be connected with six or fewer links; I wanted to see how low that number could go.

## Building the graph

Actors are nodes and shared movies are edges, which makes this an unweighted graph where breadth-first search can find shortest paths. I scraped the list of actors and their filmographies, then parallelized requests so the collection step was roughly eight times faster than a sequential pass.

The first result had a maximum separation of four. It was technically correct but not always satisfying: some paths used obscure or unreleased films. I checked release status and review counts across roughly 85,000 works, a process that took almost five hours, to prefer recognizable movies where possible.

The data then needed compression. Removing movies that were not the most popular connection between any pair of actors, reindexing everything, and keeping long names in a translation dictionary reduced the graph from nearly five megabytes to about 4.5 kB.

## Results

The maximum separation in the final graph was four degrees. One example connects Ingrid Bergman and Shah Rukh Khan; most popular contemporary actors are connected in two steps. The original interactive experiment let visitors try their own pairs, while the accompanying [GitHub project](https://github.com/Lan-Sevcnikar/ConnectingActors) contains the data and search code.
