## Environmental signals in public data

This Faculty of Computer and Information Science project investigates whether public datasets can help identify links between environmental conditions and disease development. It is an exercise in the practical limits of open data as much as it is an analysis project.

The research identifies open datasets that quantify environmental factors in a region, builds a collection and storage framework, and joins those sources into an inference pipeline. The framework was demonstrated across European regions and designed to generalize to arbitrary locations. The accompanying [Faculty publication PDF](https://erk.fe.uni-lj.si/2023/papers/sevcnikar(analiza_moznosti).pdf) provides the full methodology.
