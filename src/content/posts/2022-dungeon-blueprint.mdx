## A map-making tool for the table

Dungeon BluePrint grew from wanting a map-making tool with the exact controls I needed. It is built for easier creation of D&D maps and smoother use at the table.

The project replaces a tool I could no longer contribute to with a focused workflow for drawing maps and moving characters around during online sessions. The [live application](https://dungeonblueprint.netlify.app/) and [source on GitHub](https://github.com/LanSevcnikar/DungeonBluePrint) are still available.
